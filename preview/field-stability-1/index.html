Locating viewpoint…

The map's own camera, lowered to an eye height of 1.6 m above the map plane. That height is assumed, not measured, and the ground is flat because the map has no terrain data. Building footprints and heights come from the OpenStreetMap vector tiles the map has loaded (the tile render_height attribute, which the tileset may default where OpenStreetMap records no height). A footprint with no height attribute is drawn 9 m tall with amber outlines and counted as estimated. Doors, windows, façade detail and trees are not in the data and are not drawn. The dot texture is a rendering style, not a scan.

Signals have no measured position and stay in the tray. A bearing-only observation is drawn as a direction from the viewpoint, never as a point. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenMapTiles, OpenFreeMap.

Room POV is experimental. Demonstration Fixture: Lab 3B (8m × 6m uncalibrated enclosure). Anchor: Room Center [0, 0, 0] [Demo Fixture]. Not a surveyed anchor or real observation position.
